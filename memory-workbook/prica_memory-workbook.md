The most recent *dated* figure found was ~₦1,365 on 29 July 2026 ([CurrencyConvert](https://currencyconvert.online/usd/ngn)), with undated live quotes of ₦1,360-₦1,376. Non-USD comparable prices use assumed approximate rates (£1 = $1.30, €1 = $1.15, CA$1 = $0.73), **assumptions, not sourced rates**.

## 1. Market And Product Derivation

- **Category and format:** self-help habit workbook. Main product: **128-page US-letter paperback** (fillable pages: self-checks, daily logs, trackers). Companion: an **85-page ebook/PDF edition** (leaner).
- **Target market:** English-speaking adults 38-55 in the US, UK, Canada and Australia who are worried about everyday forgetfulness; a female skew from the perimenopause and brain-fog conversation. Buyers are in USD/GBP/CAD/AUD storefronts, not Nigeria.
- **Value proposition:** *five minutes a day, six weeks, a baseline and final self-check, no gimmicks.* Substance is real: 128 pages, six weekly modules, 24 practice exercises, 42 daily log entries.
- **Production depth that affects price:** a printed, fillable 128-page workbook is deeper than the $7-$25 printable PDFs it competes with, but it carries a print cost and the buyer expects a physical book.

## 5. Pricing Recommendation

**KDP print cost (third-party estimate, CHECK in KDP's calculator):** 8.5 x 11 in is a *large trim* (over 6.12 in wide), so a commonly cited US black-and-white rate is **$1.00 fixed + $0.017 per page**. For 128 pages that is about **$3.18 per copy**. Other sources quote $0.012 per page (about $2.54), so the true figure may be lower. KDP royalty on paperbacks is typically 60% of list price minus printing cost. These are *estimates*, not confirmed KDP figures.

### Paperback (main product)

- **Recommended price: $16.99** (**₦23,200**).
- **Defensible range: $14.99 (₦20,500) to $19.99 (₦27,300).**
  - *Floor $14.99:* the Mind Reset Guide (about $17.24 at the assumed rate) and Selar's $14.75 productivity ebook show buyers pay roughly this for a PDF or ebook habit guide; below it a 128-page print book looks cheap next to them, and the royalty drops to about $5.82.
  - *Ceiling $19.99:* the Focus + Brain Clarity Bundle at $20 and The Clarity Code at $24.99 are the highest-priced direct competitors; $19.99 stays just under them while *The Memory Workbook* lists at $29.95 for a denser, older book.
- **Estimated royalty per copy (60% of list minus print cost):** $12.99 gives about $4.62; **$14.99 about $5.82; $16.99 about $7.02; $19.99 about $8.82.** At the recommended $16.99 that is roughly **₦9,600 per copy** at ₦1,365/$ (estimate).
- **Positioning rationale:** priced in the *upper-middle* of the $7-$25 PDF cluster because the product is a physical, 128-page, structured programme with a baseline and final self-check, which none of the PDF comparables show. It sits well under the $29.95 older print book.
- **Interior ink:** a B&amp;W (greyscale) interior is assumed for the cost above. A colour interior would raise the print cost materially (rates not confirmed) and push the viable price well above $19.99, so B&amp;W is the recommended setting.

### Ebook / PDF edition (85 pages)

- **Recommended price: $7.99** (**₦10,900**). **Range: $6.99 (₦9,500) to $9.99 (₦13,600).** This sits inside the Kindle 70% band ($2.99-$12.99, effective 7 July 2026, [KDP](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634560)) and in line with the cheapest direct competitors ($7 Brain Fog Breaker Reset, £6.99 Brain Fog Reset). Royalty at 70% is about **$5.59 before delivery fees**.
- **Format caution:** the workbook is designed to be written in. The ebook/PDF edition is a convenience version; it is a weaker product than the paperback, so keep it clearly cheaper.

### Platform-specific notes
- **One global USD price is more defensible** than differentiated pricing: all comparable evidence is USD-denominated and the buyers are in USD/GBP/CAD/AUD markets. Let Amazon convert to local storefronts (check each marketplace's prices).
- **Selar / naira pricing:** only worth a separate naira listing for a Nigerian audience, and no comparable evidence supports a specific price: Selar comparables here are finance or productivity ebooks, with ₦9,900 as the only naira anchor.

### Risk flags
- **No verified sales evidence** for any comparable; the price band rests on listing prices only.
- **Print cost is a third-party estimate** and Amazon changes rates, so recompute the royalty in KDP before locking the price.
- **Exchange-rate risk:** the ₦/$ rate is volatile; the ₦ column is indicative only. KDP pays Nigerian authors by wire transfer in NGN (verified 2026-09-23; confirm current terms).
- **Review moat:** established brain-health books have thousands of ratings (e.g. Goodreads shows tens of thousands for *Limitless* and *Unlimited Memory*); a new title starts with none, so early price promotions may be needed.
- **Competitive crowding:** low-priced PDFs ($7) can anchor buyers low for the ebook edition.
