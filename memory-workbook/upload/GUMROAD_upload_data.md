## 3. Description (paste into the rich-text editor)

```
Forgot why you walked into the room? Lost your keys again?

Everyday forgetfulness is common in midlife, and it is usually about attention, sleep, stress and overload. The 5-Minute Memory Workbook gives you a calm, practical way to work on it: five minutes a day, for six weeks.

Each week adds one tiny habit, tied to something you already do every day. You write your plan, track your progress and see for yourself what changes.

WHAT YOU GET
- The printable workbook (PDF, 129 pages including the cover, US letter size)
- A digital edition (PDF, 86 pages) for tablets and phones, without the extra log pages

WHAT'S INSIDE
- A 6-week programme with one tiny habit per week: a sleep wind-down, single-tasking, movement, recall practice and a simple way to tag names and places
- A baseline self-check at the start and a final self-check at the end, so you can compare your own results
- 24 practice exercises, 42 daily log entries, weekly reviews and a 12-week tracker
- If-then planners that tie each habit to your real routine
- A memory slip log, a spaced recall planner and 40 more tiny habits to choose from
- A plain-language page on when to talk to a doctor

WHO IT'S FOR
Busy adults, roughly 40 to 55, who want a more reliable everyday memory without memory palaces, gimmicks or guilt. It suits professionals, parents, and anyone noticing brain fog.

HOW TO USE IT
Print the workbook at 100% on US letter paper (on A4, choose "fit to page"), or fill in the digital edition with a PDF annotation app. Start with the self-check, then take one week at a time.

This is a practical habit workbook, not medical advice. If you are worried about your memory, please talk to your doctor.
```
