# Louda Pack: The 5-Minute Memory Workbook

**Product:** The 5-Minute Memory Workbook: Tiny Daily Habits for a Sharper, More Reliable Mind After 40 (Cimple Books). Paperback workbook, 128 pages, plus a lean ebook.

**Evidence note:** The research below came from a handful of web searches. It found creator and product listings, but **no verified view counts or sales figures**. Everything marked *proxy* is inferred from what was visible, not measured. Treat the ranking of hooks and formats as a starting hypothesis to test with real posts.

## 1. Niche Derivation

- **Category:** self-help habit workbook (health and mind).
- **Niche:** a gentle, evidence-informed memory-and-focus habit workbook for busy adults in midlife, including people experiencing perimenopausal brain fog.
- **Buyer persona:** a time-poor 40-something who keeps losing keys, names and trains of thought, is worried but not in crisis, and wants something practical and non-medical.
- **Positioning angle:** small, specific, reassuring. *Five minutes a day, six weeks, no gimmicks.*
- **Key target market (content speaks to first):** English-speaking women and men aged 38-50 in the US, UK, Canada and Australia (the stores where the paperback sells), with a **female skew** in the perimenopause and brain-fog conversation. The buyer is the user.

## 2. Niche Content Framework Scan

What turned up (proxy; no creator metrics available):
- **Meno TikTok is large.** A CBC report says menopause-hashtag videos have recorded over a billion views on TikTok in total ([CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/1.6861886)). That is the whole category, not any single video.
- **Recurring pillars:** symptom lists that go beyond hot flushes, relatable humour about mid-sentence blanks, and reassurance that it is a hormonal or life-stage change and not a personal failing (summarised from the same search).
- **Credibility gap:** one guide warns that menopause hashtags carry a lot of unreliable content ([Chatelaine](https://www.chatelaine.com/health/menopause-tiktok/)), so creators who pair personal experience with medical grounding stand out.
- **Storytelling shapes seen:** relatable-moment humour, reassurance myth-busting, and "things nobody tells you" listicles.
- **Brain-health content also exists as a product category:** a brand recruiting creators for "memory recall" and productivity-hack content on TikTok and Instagram shows that memory is a commercial content theme.
