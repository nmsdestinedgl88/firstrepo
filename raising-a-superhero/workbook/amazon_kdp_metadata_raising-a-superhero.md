```html
<p><b>Their biggest challenges may be hiding their biggest strengths.</b></p><p>Intense focus. Endless energy. Racing ideas. Big feelings. If you are raising a child with ADHD, you have probably heard the word "too" a lot. Many kids with ADHD have traits that cause friction at home and could grow into real strengths. The hard part is knowing what to <i>do</i> about it, and keeping your own patience from running dry while you do.</p><p><b>Raising a Superhero</b> is a workbook for parents, with simple activities to do together, most of them 10 to 20 minutes, using ordinary household items. It is not medical, psychological or educational advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk with your child's qualified professionals about diagnosis, treatment and school support.</i></p>
```

Every claim above matches the finished file: 40 missions, 8 modules, a Guardian Pause in each mission, 29 phrase swaps and praise lines in the Phrase Bank (12 + 11 + 6), and the Patience Toolkit, tracker and Wins Log pages.
