# 1. Niche Derivation

- **Category:** parenting and neurodiversity workbook, print-first, non-medical.
- **Specific niche:** a strengths-based activity workbook for parents of children about 5 to 12 who have ADHD, with a built-in parent-patience practice.
- **Buyer persona (inferred):** a parent in their 30s or 40s, often the one managing mornings, homework and school contact, who is tired, wants something to do tonight and does not want to be shamed. I found no buyer-demographic data, so gender and ethnicity are not asserted.
- **Buyer versus user:** the parent buys and uses it, with the child taking part in the activities. The child does not read the book.
- **Positioning angle:** transformation in small steps ("simple things to do tonight") plus relief for the parent ("grow your patience"). Not a clinical or diagnostic angle.
- **Key target market:** English-speaking parents shopping Amazon, US first (the Kindle Ranker and Apple search data in the market report are US), then UK, Canada and Australia. A direct-sales channel in Nigeria is a secondary market, with Naira pricing from prica.

# 7. Instagram Reel Templates

All three use Maya as presenter. Each is an original structure inspired by the patterns in section 4. Hook, topic and format combinations are all different. Children appear only as hands or the back of the head. Keep captions to practical tips and add "not medical advice".

## Template 1: "The Ten-Minute Fix"
- **Built from:** topic 1 (daily friction) + hook 1 (problem, then fix) + format 1 (talking to camera with a prop). It mirrors the most consistent pattern in the evidence.
- **Alternative hooks:** "Stopping the game should not feel like a fire alarm." / "The transition is the hard part, so I plan the landing." / "Same five words, three warnings, no shouting."
- **Beats (about 20 seconds):** 0-3s hook (Clip 1) | 3-9s the timer on the table (Clip 2) | 9-15s the book on the counter (Clip 3, plus REAL footage of Mission 1.3) | 15-20s CTA (Clip 4, plus the real cover end card).
- **On-screen text:** "Stopping the game = fire alarm" / "10 - 5 - 1" / "Mission 1.3: The Gentle Landing".
- **Caption:** "Transitions are where our evenings fall apart, so we practise the landing: ten minutes, five minutes, one minute. It is Mission 1.3 in Raising a Superhero. Not medical advice, just something to try tonight. Link in bio."
- **CTA:** "Link in bio for the workbook." **Length:** 18 to 22 seconds.

## Template 2: "Say This Instead"
- **Built from:** topic 2 (patience and relationships) + hook 3 (a curiosity or "reverse-engineer" opener) + format 3 (a swap listicle told with on-screen text). It is the patience pillar from the research.
- **Alternative hooks:** "Three phrases I swapped, and my mornings got calmer." / "I stopped saying hurry up. Here is what I say now." / "The five-second pause that stops me before I snap."
- **Beats (about 16 seconds):** 0-5s first swap (Clip 5) | 5-11s the Guardian Pause (Clip 6) | 11-16s Phrase Bank plug (Clip 7, plus REAL footage of the Phrase Bank page) | end card.
- **On-screen text:** "Instead of: Hurry up!" / "Try: We leave in five minutes. What's left?" / "5-second pause".

## Template 3: "Not a Bad Attention Span"
- **Built from:** topic 3 (strengths reframing) + hook 2 (a self-deprecating admission) + format 2 (a short story told as a day-in-the-life moment). It carries the strengths angle that the book sells on.
