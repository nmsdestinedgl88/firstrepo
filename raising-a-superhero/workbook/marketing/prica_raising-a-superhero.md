# 1. Market and Product Derivation

- **Format:** print-first paperback activity workbook (144 pages, 7 x 10 in, black-and-white interior) with a Kindle companion and a printable PDF.
- **Target market:** English-speaking parents of children about 5 to 12 with ADHD, shopping on Amazon first (US, then UK, Canada, Australia). A Nigeria-direct channel is secondary.
- **Value proposition:** forty simple activities plus a built-in parent-patience practice; non-medical.
- **Production depth that affects price expectations:** 144 pages, 40 activities, a Patience Toolkit, Phrase Bank, 30-Day Tracker and Wins Log. The kid-workbook competitors run 96 to 184 pages.

# 5. Pricing Recommendation

## Paperback (lead product)

- **Recommended price:** **$16.99 (₦23,200)**.
- **Defensible range:** floor **$14.99 (₦20,450)**, bounded by SpreadLife's ODD workbook at $14.91 (rank 26,184), the lowest price among the top-ranked workbook-style titles. Ceiling **$17.99 (₦24,550)**, bounded by Pisciotta at $17.99, Khang at $17.95 and the Thriving workbook at $17.99. Avoid $19.99 and above, where the two $21.67 to $21.95 titles show weaker signals.
- **Positioning:** upper-middle of the strongest cluster ($16.48 to $17.99). Laine, the top title, is priced at $16.48; $16.99 matches the depth of a 144-page workbook with 40 activities against the 96 to 184-page workbook set.
- **Launch option (a judgment, not evidence):** list at $14.99 for the first weeks to earn early reviews, then move to $16.99. A new book has no review moat, and the top comparables have 250 to 500 reviews.

### Margin check (KDP, from KDP's own printing-cost and royalty pages)

7 x 10 in is KDP "Large" trim. Black ink, 144 pages: printing cost = $1.00 + $0.017 x 144 = **$3.45**. Royalty is 60% of list price above $9.98 minus printing cost (50% at $9.98 or below). Expanded Distribution pays 40%. KDP did not show an effective date on these tables, so re-check in the dashboard.

| List price | ₦ | Royalty (60%) | Royalty (Expanded Distribution, 40%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| $14.99 | ₦20,450 | $5.55 (₦7,550) | $2.55 (₦3,500) |
| $16.99 | ₦23,200 | $6.75 (₦9,200) | $3.35 (₦4,550) |
| $17.99 | ₦24,550 | $7.35 (₦10,050) | $3.75 (₦5,100) |

**Colour interior is not viable at this price:** premium colour on Large trim would cost $1.00 + $0.08 x 144 = $12.52 per copy, so choose black-and-white.

## Kindle companion

- **Recommended:** **$6.99 (₦9,550)**; range $4.99 to $9.99 (₦6,800 to ₦13,650). All inside the verified 70% band of $2.99 to $12.99 (effective 7 July 2026).
- **Why:** the Kindle median in the ADHD and hyperactivity categories is $9.00 to $9.99 (third-party estimate), but a fill-in workbook has less value on a screen and a print edition will anchor the value. $9.99 is the ceiling; $0.99 deal pricing ranked poorly in the comparables.
- **Royalty:** about $4.74 per sale at $6.99 (70% minus an assumed $0.15 delivery cost; delivery cost is an assumption to check on KDP).

## Printable PDF, direct sale

- **Recommended:** **$12.00 (₦16,400)**; range $9.00 to $19.00 (₦12,300 to ₦25,950).
- **Bounds:** floor at the $9 Gumroad guide; ceiling below the $25 to $30 Gumroad bundles and $28 to $29 Payhip toolkits, which contain more separate files than a single workbook. Etsy listings span $2.98 to $24.99.
- **Evidence limit:** none of these listings showed sales, so this price rests on listing positions only. No Selar comparable was found, so I cannot support a naira-denominated price for Nigerian buyers from evidence. If you sell locally, check Selar listings first and consider a lower naira price point as a test.

## Platform notes and risk flags

- **Single global price or differentiated?** Amazon and Gumroad/Payhip are USD-denominated; keep one USD price per product. A separate naira price on a local platform should be tested, not assumed.
- **Risk flags:** (1) exchange rate: approximation, with sources ranging widely; (2) rank signals are estimates; (3) thin direct-sales evidence (no sales counts); (4) the top titles have large review counts that a new book lacks; (5) KDP printing and royalty tables had no effective date shown; (6) the Teachable programs ($299 to $499) are a different product tier and are not used to set the workbook price.
